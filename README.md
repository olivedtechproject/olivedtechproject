# Better Together Bridge Builder

Can you vibe code a website that people who have been in a conflict can go to try to solve it. There would be different pages. I like Reddit AITAH, where kids can go to voice their opinions while also giving feedback to other stories. A directional page that is a chart/filter that gives suggestions on how to regulate and channel emotions, e.g., lie down and listen to music. And a page that would be a place for reaching out to the other person in the conflict. You email them how you viewed the situation, what you are feeling, and a gift/appreciation or something you would like to do in the future for that person. Hopefully, starting a way for a conflict to resolve.
